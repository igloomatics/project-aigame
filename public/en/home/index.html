Welcome to the ZGCA AI&Game Lab website! We are devoted to exploring the future combination of games and AI. We regard games as the best training ground for AI evolution, use AI to reshape game development productivity, and are simultaneously committed to breaking script limitations to bring a personalized intelligent interaction experience to players. We are affiliated with Beijing Zhongguancun Academy.

Game for AI

Regard games as benchmark to test AI capability boundaries. Including but not limited to: Reinforcement Learning, Multi-Agent Systems, Large Language Models, Multimodal Models, Embodied AI, etc.

AI for Game

Leverage AI to create game development tools, improving quality and reducing costs in game development and operations. Including but not limited to: 2D/3D Art Asset Development, Unity/UE Code Agents, Game Marketing Video Generation, etc.

AI in Game

Create fun AI-native games with the goal of future commercialization. Similar products on the market: Pepper Simulator, 1001 Nights, etc.
